## 💡 Tips for Success:

1. **Start Simple**: Begin with basic aggregates before moving to complex groupings
2. **Test Incrementally**: Build queries step by step
3. **Verify Results**: Check that your numbers make business sense
4. **Optimize**: Look for ways to make queries more efficient
5. **Document**: Add comments to explain complex logic
